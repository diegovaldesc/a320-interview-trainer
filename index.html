ICAO English

Each test is a single round that randomly mixes multiple choice, audio, picture description and a role-play with ATC. You get one at random, and it won't repeat until you've done the others. No grade or level is given here: compare with the model and move on.

The audio is read by the English voices on your device: each clip uses a different voice, chosen at random and always at the same speed; quality depends on your phone or browser. The microphone only transcribes what you say and never calculates a grade or an ICAO level. If you leave in the middle of a test you can continue it later; the exercise you were on starts over.
